# Users

One short block per role. `roles.md` says what each can do; this says who they are.

## The person who pays

Their title, what a good day looks like, what a bad one looks like, and the moment they would open this product.

## The person who uses it most

Often not the payer. What they do on day one, what they do every day, and the one thing that would make them stop.

## The person on the outside

A customer, a client, a member of the public. What they see, what they never see, and whether they need an account at all.

## What each must never see

One line per role. Another organisation's data is the obvious one. There are usually two more.
