# Product brief

A brief that needs more is a brief that is not settled.

## The one sentence

Who it is for, what it does, and why they would pay. It is the same sentence the landing page opens with.

## Who it is for

The person who decides to pay. Their job, their day, what they use today for this.

## The actual problem

What goes wrong for them now, in their words. A number if there is one: hours lost, revenue missed, customers who did not come back.

## What has to be true

The three or four things that, if false, mean the product should not be built. Each one is checkable.

## What v1 is not

The features people will ask for that are out. Written down so nobody relitigates them at week three.

## How it gets its first user

Named, if possible. The one team, the one shop, the one person who has said yes to trying it.
