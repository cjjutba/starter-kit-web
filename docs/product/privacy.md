# Privacy

This feeds `src/content/privacy.ts`, and the two must agree. RA 10173 applies to data about people in the Philippines.

## What is collected

One line per kind of data, and which screen collects it.

## Why

One line per kind. If there is no why, do not collect it.

## How long it is kept

Account data, product data, logs, abuse counters, deletion requests. A number for each. The kit ships with thirty days for mail, a day for counters and a year for a request.

## Who can see it

Roles inside the organisation. The team running the product. Any processor, named, with a link to their notice. The kit ships with four: Neon, Vercel with its cookieless analytics, Resend, and Sentry with personal data switched off. Remove one here when it is removed from the product.
