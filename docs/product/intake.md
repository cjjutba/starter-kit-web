# Intake

The brief as it was pasted and every question and answer that followed,
kept whole because cutting either loses context. `/plan` condenses it into
the other product docs. Append only. When an answer changes, add the new one under the old with
the date, so the reasoning survives.

## The brief

Paste it whole, as sent. An amendment goes under its own heading with the
date, and the later one wins where they conflict.

## Questions and answers

One question and one answer per pair, grouped by the round they were asked
in, with the date. Write the answer in the person's words. A question the
person declined to answer stays here marked open, and no skill wires an
open item.

## Open

The questions still unanswered when the person said nothing else was open.
Each one names the skill or feature that will need it.
