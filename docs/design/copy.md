# Copy

How the interface talks. The `unslop` rules apply here too.

## Voice

Plain, direct, warm without being cute. Second person. Short sentences. It says what happened and what to do next.

## Buttons

A verb and its object: "Save note", "Send invitation", "Sign in". Never "Submit", never "OK". The loading label is the verb in progress: "Saving", "Sending".

## Errors

Say what went wrong and what to do, in one line, next to the field that caused it. Never blame the person. Never a red banner. "That password is not right. Try again or reset it."

## Empty states

A heading that names what is missing and one line that says how the first one arrives. Then the action.

## Numbers, dates and times

Tabular figures in columns. Dates as "Fri 5 Sep" or "5 September 2026". Times as "9:30 AM PHT" with the zone whenever it could be misread.
