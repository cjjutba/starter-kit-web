# Visual direction

How the look was decided, what was tried, and what it cost. `DESIGN.md` records the result as numbers. This records the reasoning.

## What was decided

The taste, in a paragraph. Starter-kit ships the near white and near black direction Kalinga settled. Say whether this product keeps it or changes it, and what changes. The four knobs in `DESIGN.md` under "Making it yours", accent, ground, shape and typeface, are set at setup or here. Say which moved, to what, and why.

## What was tried, in order

One line per board or reference: what it was, the model and price if generated, and why it lost or won. The boards live in `explorations/`.

## Where the warmth comes from

Photography, illustration, the mark, or nothing. This is the one question the monochrome direction forces.
